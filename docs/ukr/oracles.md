# Ukrainian gold-data oracles

The oracle pair for the Ukrainian verification loop, chosen by the same
criterion as the other languages: two machine-readable sources of
independent provenance, so their agreement is strong evidence and their
disagreements form the adjudication corpus.

## Why this pair

UniMorph uk is out for the usual reason: it is an English-Wiktionary
scrape, so it shares kaikki's lineage and its agreement would be circular.

1. **kaikki.org Ukrainian** (en.wiktionary `uk-conj` templates via
   Wiktextract; CC BY-SA / GFDL). The tables expand cleanly.
   `scripts/ukr/fetch_kaikki.sh` → `data/ukr/kaikki.tsv`: 99,073 rows,
   5,501 lemmas.
2. **VESUM** (brown-uk/dict_uk, the LanguageTool Ukrainian source; the
   expanded full-form dictionary `dict_corp_vis.txt` shipped in release
   v6.8.0, GPL/LGPL/MPL — *not* a Wiktionary derivative).
   `scripts/ukr/fetch_vesum.sh` (release-pinned, sha256-checked) →
   `data/ukr/vesum.tsv`: 904,589 rows, 46,260 lemmas.

The two agree on **71,990 of 72,319** shared (lemma, feature) slots —
**99.55%**. The 329 disagreements are genuine orthographic doublets
(prefix variants *упретеся*/*увіпретеся*, stem doublets
*відволоче*/*відволіче*, contracted pasts *вдяглися*/*вдягнулися*) and
are excluded from gold, not scored.

## Feature schema

The same `lemma ⇥ form ⇥ features` TSV as the other languages. Ukrainian
slots:

- `V;NFIN` — the infinitive.
- `V;IND;PRS;{SG,PL};{1,2,3}` — the non-past finite paradigm. This is one
  morphological slot set: the imperfective present (*читаю*) and the
  perfective non-past, which carries future meaning (*прочитаю*) and which
  both oracles tag "future". The imperfective synthetic future in -тиму
  (*читатиму*) is a distinct formation and is not this slot.
- `V;IND;PST;{MASC,FEM,NEUT};SG` and `V;IND;PST;PL` — the l-past marks
  gender in the singular (*читав/читала/читало*) and only number in the
  plural (*читали*).
- `V;IMP;{SG,PL};{1,2,3}` — the imperative (2sg, 1pl, 2pl exist).

Perfective and imperfective are distinct lemmas. Combining stress marks
(U+0301) are stripped from the kaikki forms so they compare against the
unstressed VESUM lexicon. Reflexive (-ся) verbs are kept as their own
lemmas; the -ся/-сь and -тися/-тись/-ться spellings are variants that the
harness unions.

## The engine

Two conjugations. The productive first conjugation with a vowel stem
(*читати → читаю*, *купувати → купую*) and the second conjugation with its
1sg/3pl consonant mutations (*робити → роблю/роблять*, *сидіти →
сиджу/сидять*, *носити → ношу/носять*) are generated by rule; the l-past
and imperative derive from the infinitive. Every stem-changing or
athematic verb — the athematic consonant-stem pasts (*нести → ніс*), the
prefixed suppletives, the vowel-alternating and mutating presents the
rules do not predict — is an explicit row in `data/ukr/verbs.tsv`, mined
from the oracle agreement: `scripts/ukr/capture_irregulars.sh` records the
verbs the rule engine misses and `scripts/ukr/mine_verbs.py` fills their
paradigms from the kaikki ∩ VESUM merge.
