# Maltese: skipped — kaikki tables do not expand

Ġabra (mlrs.research.um.edu.mt, CC BY) is a genuinely open,
full-paradigm Maltese lexicon — better than several oracles this
project ships with. But Wiktextract expands **3 of 7,315** kaikki
Maltese verb entries (the Polish failure mode), leaving Ġabra alone:
a single-oracle loop. The Semitic templatic engine would also be the
most expensive in the EU-24 for the smallest agreement set. Parked.
